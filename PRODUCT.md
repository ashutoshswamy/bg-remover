# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Everyday people who need a transparent PNG fast: sellers listing products, students, social posters. No signup, no watermark, no learning curve.

## Product Purpose
Matte removes image backgrounds entirely in the browser using @imgly/background-removal. Success: user drops a photo, gets a clean transparent PNG in seconds, trusts that nothing left their device.

## Positioning
Free, on-device background removal. Photos never upload to any server, which hosted competitors cannot truthfully claim.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Up to 5 images per session (MAX_IMAGES = 5), processed client-side via dynamic import of the wasm model.
- Per-image states: pending, processing, done, error. Download as `no-bg-<name>.png`.
- Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind v4, single page.
- Toasts must use sileo.
- Fonts restricted to Bricolage Grotesque and DM Sans only.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Matte. Keep URL https://mattebgremover.ashutoshswamy.in and SEO metadata (title, description, OG image).
- Footer credits Ashutosh Swamy with links: Portfolio, GitHub, LinkedIn, X.
- Privacy claim: nothing leaves the browser.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, customer logos, or metrics exist. Do not fabricate. public/og-image.png exists.

## Product Principles
- Privacy is the product: say it plainly, show it works.
- Zero friction: drop, wait, download.
- Honest states: progress, errors and limits always visible.
